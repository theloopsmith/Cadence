Clinical Pharmacokinetics

Protecting your ON-state from protein.

Dietary protein breaks down into large neutral amino acids that compete with Levodopa at the LAT1 transporter across the blood-brain barrier. Taking Levodopa alongside a high-protein meal causes delayed ON or sudden motor wearing-off. Cadence alerts wearers before timing conflicts arise.
